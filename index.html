ShulkerBox

Instant Mod Switcher

A lightweight Windows utility designed to store, manage, and hot-swap Minecraft mod profiles instantly without full game reinstalls or complex launcher setups.

Download for Windows
One-click activation into your vanilla directory
Cleans up cryptic file names into readable titles
Export custom .shulker packages to share with friends
Completely standalone with no local installers needed
Open source project on https://github.com/compot2600/ShulkerBox
